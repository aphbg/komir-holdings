Five-Layer Founder Protection
Architecture locked Aug 2026
1
IP Origination

Every system, methodology, and architecture in the portfolio was created by the founder as personal intellectual property before being licensed to the holding company. The Capital Engine, every venture architecture, every operational system — all originated with the founder and are formally documented. The company operates on licensed IP. The IP belongs to the creator.

FOUNDER IP
2
Dual-Class Share Structure

Two classes of shares. Class A carries standard voting rights. Class B carries superior voting rights (10:1 or higher) and is held exclusively by the founder and designated trust structures. Class B shares are non-transferable and non-dilutable. Economic ownership can shift — voting control does not.

CONSTITUTIONAL
3
Founder Reserved Matters

A defined list of decisions that require the founder's written consent regardless of board vote or shareholder majority. No resolution on these matters passes without the founder's signature. The list is embedded in the company's constitutional documents.

FOUNDER VETO
4
Irrevocable Trust

The family holding structure is held within an irrevocable trust. The trust holds Class B shares and the IP portfolio. It protects the structure from all parties — including the founder. The trustee's fiduciary duty is to the beneficiaries across generations.

TRUST STRUCTURE
5
Golden Share

A single share with the power to block any resolution that would alter the company's fundamental character, ownership structure, or founder protections. Even if every other shareholder and the board votes unanimously — the golden share holds final authority.

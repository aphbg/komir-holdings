The Flywheel
How capital moves
ENGINE
Proprietary trading system generates returns across global financial markets. Operates continuously and independently.
TREASURY
Director of Capital & Treasury receives output. Allocates across ventures. Parks reserves in treasury instruments between deployments.
VENTURES
Each venture funded by internal capital allocation. No debt, no external equity at this stage. Venture launches and scales on sovereign capital.
REVENUE
Ventures generate revenue. Reinvests in the venture or flows back to the holding company. The portfolio becomes self-sustaining.
COMPOUND
Returns flow back to treasury. Capital base grows. Engine scales. Next venture launches with a larger foundation. The cycle accelerates.
Commercial Architecture
Three layers
LAYER 1
Private Engine

The core proprietary system. Runs on dedicated infrastructure. Never sold, never shared. Methodologies and execution systems remain inside Komir permanently.

LAYER 2
Licensed Indicators

Commercial trading tools derived from the engine's methodology. Revenue-generating without exposing the core system. Distributed through established platforms.

LAYER 3
Managed Capital

Structured desks for institutional and HNWI partners. Managed within Komir infrastructure. Escrow custody, transparent reporting, performance-based fees.

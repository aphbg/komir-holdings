KOMIR HOLDINGS

Sovereign capital.
The engine that funds everything.

Komir Holdings is powered by a proprietary trading system designed, built, and owned by its founder. The Capital Engine removes external dependency from every venture in the portfolio — and creates the foundation for funding businesses, industries, and incubation programmes across Africa.

THE CAPITAL ENGINE

A proprietary trading system. Not a fund. A production system.

The Capital Engine is a proprietary, systematic trading operation. It operates across global financial markets using methodologies and algorithms designed by the founder as personal intellectual property — licensed to the holding company under a formal agreement.

It is not a hedge fund. It is not an investment vehicle open to outside capital. It is a production system that generates capital on demand — the internal equivalent of a revenue-generating business, except its product is capital itself. The engine scales with the capital base, and the capital base grows with the portfolio.

THE FLYWHEEL

How sovereign capital moves through the portfolio.

Capital flows in one direction — from the engine, through the treasury, into ventures, and back. Every cycle makes the next one larger. No external dependency at any point in the loop.

ENGINE
The proprietary trading system generates returns across global financial markets. The engine operates continuously — independent of any single venture's performance.
TREASURY
The Director of Capital & Treasury receives engine output and allocates across the portfolio. Which ventures need capital, when, in what form. Reserves are parked in treasury instruments between deployments.
VENTURES
Each venture is funded by internal capital allocation — not by debt or external equity. The venture launches, operates, and scales with sovereign capital. No dilution, no external timelines.
REVENUE
Ventures generate their own revenue. That revenue either reinvests in the venture or flows back to the holding company as returns. The portfolio becomes self-sustaining.
COMPOUND
Returns flow back to the treasury. The capital base grows. The engine scales. The next venture launches with a larger foundation than the last. The cycle accelerates.
SOVEREIGN BY DESIGN

External capital enters from strength. Never from necessity.

When external capital enters the portfolio — through partnerships, equity, or institutional relationships — it enters on Komir's terms. The ventures are already funded, already operational, already generating returns. The negotiation starts from a position where the capital is useful but not necessary.

This means no dilution pressure, no pivot mandates, no board seats traded for runway. The founder retains structural control through governance architecture, and the Capital Engine ensures that no venture ever depends on an external cheque to survive.

COMMERCIAL ARCHITECTURE

Three layers. Internal engine, licensed tools, managed capital.

LAYER 1
Private Engine

The core proprietary system. Runs on dedicated infrastructure. Never sold, never shared. The methodologies, algorithms, and execution systems remain inside Komir infrastructure permanently. This is the asset that funds everything.

LAYER 2
Licensed Indicators

Commercial trading tools derived from the engine's methodology — licensed to external traders and institutions. Revenue-generating without exposing the core system. Available through established distribution platforms.

LAYER 3
Managed Capital

Structured capital desks for institutional and high-net-worth partners. Managed within Komir infrastructure using the proprietary system. Escrow custody, transparent reporting, and performance-based fee structures.

BEYOND TRADING

Funding ventures, incubating industries, deploying capital across Africa.

The Capital Engine is the starting point — not the entire story. As the portfolio matures, the capital function expands into direct venture funding, industry-specific incubation programmes, and strategic capital deployment across the continent.

Komir will fund businesses and ventures across Africa — within and outside its own industries. Industry-specific incubator cohorts are brought on-site for three to six months of mentorship, development, and funding. The portfolio's infrastructure, expertise, and capital are deployed to build the next generation of companies across the sectors that matter most.

This is not venture capital as the industry practices it. There are no pitch decks, no demo days, no spray-and-pray. Komir identifies the infrastructure gap in an industry, builds alongside the founders who can close it, and deploys capital from a system that generates it internally. The relationship starts with capability, not a term sheet.

PEOPLE

People

Every role in the holding company, who it reports to, and what it pays. Compensation is designed so that joining Komir Holdings from anywhere in the world is a career upgrade, not a sacrifice. Housing, transport, meals, wellness, and childcare are infrastructure — not perks.

The Standard

Komir Holdings invests in its people at the level that makes relocation to Nigeria a career upgrade. A person leaving London, Singapore, or New York arrives into a standard they recognise — compensation, housing support, security, wellness, nutrition, and education for their children.

Nobody at Komir earns less than they would at the best company in Nigeria for the same role. Emotional regulation is a condition of employment. Therapy is provided. The system is designed so that bad behaviour is structurally impossible — every role has an agent running alongside it, every output is visible, every contribution is measurable.

People Principles
Agent-First (Doctrine IX)

Every role has an agent before it has a human. When the human arrives, the agent becomes their tool. The system works before you do.

First-Hire Doctrine

One person enters doing everything. They ascend as the next arrives. Character over credentials. Wholeness over intelligence.

Promote from Within

The holding company promotes from its ventures. Venture leaders become holding company executives. External specialist hires only where specific qualifications are required.

Emotional Regulation Required

Therapy is provided. Emotional disregulation disqualifies. The company invests in your wellbeing. You maintain your capacity.

Equity Participation

Long-tenure employees earn equity. The ESOP vests over time. You benefit from what you build. Founder control is protected by dual-class share structure.

Transparency by Design

Every role's compensation is visible. Every output is measurable. The system makes bad behaviour structurally impossible — not through policing, but through architecture.

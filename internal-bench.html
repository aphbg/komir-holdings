The Bench

Talent pipeline for every role across the holding company and ventures. Screening criteria, role mapping, and the First-Hire Doctrine as the evaluation framework.

The First-Hire Doctrine

One person enters doing everything. They ascend as the next arrives. Nobody is ever brought in above someone who grew from the ground. Character over credentials. Wholeness over intelligence. A brilliant person who cannot be trusted is less valuable than a capable person who can do all three — trust, collaborate, grow.

Screening Questions
Applied to every candidate
What have you built that nobody asked you to build?
Tests initiative — do they wait for instructions or create value unprompted?
Tell me about a time you were wrong and what you did about it.
Tests integrity and self-awareness — do they own mistakes or deflect?
What would you do in your first 30 days if nobody gave you a task?
Tests self-direction — can they operate without a manager hovering?
Who in your life would say you made their life better, and how?
Tests wholeness — are they building people around them or just themselves?
What's something you believe about work that most people disagree with?
Tests independent thinking — do they have conviction or just consensus?
If this role disappeared in six months, what would you have left behind?
Tests legacy thinking — do they build systems or just fill seats?

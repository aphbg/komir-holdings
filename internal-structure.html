Personal Staff Layer
4 roles protecting founder capacity
Chief of Staff

Founder's brain externalised. Decision queue, cross-venture synthesis, information filtering. Every other staff role reports here.

Executive Assistant

Business brain. Schedule, communications, operational rhythm. First hire in the personal staff sequence. Reports to CoS.

PA / Aide-de-Camp

Movement, travel, physical security, logistics. The founder moves between locations without friction.

House Manager

Domestic SOPs. Home, meals, maintenance, household staff, personal logistics. Non-work life runs without attention.

Campus Masterplan

A campus of interconnected buildings designed for one purpose: an environment where exceptional people build, trade, learn, recover, and compound — all within walking distance, all under one standard, all owned by Komir Holdings.

THE CAMPUS

Thirteen buildings. One standard.

Each building serves a distinct function. All are interconnected by walkways, green corridors, and shared infrastructure. The campus operates 24 hours.

The capital engine of Komir Holdings. A secure, purpose-built facility housing the proprietary trading operation, treasury, capital allocation, and the Komir incubator programme. Designed for focus, speed, and precision — soundproofed, climate-controlled, and equipped for extended sessions.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

The standard for every Komir space.

24-hour functionality

The campus never closes. Different zones serve different hours. The trading floor follows global markets. The hub serves the public around the clock. The school operates daytime. The wellness centre is always available.

Recovery is infrastructure

Sleep, movement, decompression, and access to nature are not benefits. They are operational requirements engineered into every building and every walkway on the campus.

Parents don't choose

Childcare and education are on campus. No team member chooses between their work and their family. The child learns at Praxis. The parent builds at Jovilex. They are 200 metres apart.

Green is structural

Plants, herbs, grass, and natural light are not decoration. They are design requirements in every building and every outdoor space. People put their feet on grass during their workday.

One standard, applied globally

What is built here defines the DNA for every Komir workspace worldwide. This campus is the reference point. Every future location meets this standard or does not carry the name.

HOW WE GET THERE

Four phases. Each one funds the next.

PHASE 1 · NOW
Warehouse — Nigeria

A functional commercial space. The entire company starts here — building, trading, operating, training, eating, exercising. Everything under one roof until ventures grow enough to need their own locations.

PHASE 2 · 2027
Lagos Floor

A single premium floor for market presence. Client-facing operations, trading desks, coworking, fitness, recovery, nursery. Self-funded. Establishes the design DNA every future space inherits.

PHASE 3 · 2028–2030+
Global HQ Campus

The full thirteen-building campus. Externally financed from proven operations. All ventures consolidated under one site. The Founder's Office sits within as a personal command centre.

PHASE 4 · GENERATIONAL
Jolag Farm City

Large-scale agricultural city — food production, processing, housing, and industry. Institutional finance engaged. The billion-dollar layer — a city built around food and industry.

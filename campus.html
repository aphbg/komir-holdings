CAMPUS PRINCIPLES

The standard for every Komir space.

Zero Diesel, Zero Grid

Solar arrays and lithium battery banks. Data centre at 72-hour autonomous capacity. No generator. No fuel. No dependency.

One Design Language

A single document governs every surface, material, and detail across every building. The standard is set once and replicated everywhere.

Family-First Infrastructure

Childcare, education, wellness, and nutrition built into the campus as infrastructure, not perks. The system works when people are whole.

One Nutrition Standard

Every meal on campus comes through Elruta. What enters the body is designed, not chosen from a menu.

Six Workstreams

Architecture, interior, landscape, structural/civil, MEP/smart systems, project management. Each led by a strategic principal.

Serve Then Commercialise

Every building serves the portfolio first. Revenue from the public, standard set by internal demand.

HOW WE GET THERE

Four phases. Each one funds the next.

PHASE 1 · NOW
Warehouse — Nigeria

A functional commercial space. The entire company starts here — building, trading, operating, training, eating, exercising. Everything under one roof until ventures grow enough to need their own locations.

PHASE 2 · 2027
Lagos Floor

A single premium floor for market presence. Client-facing operations, trading desks, coworking, fitness, recovery, nursery. Self-funded. Establishes the design DNA every future space inherits.

PHASE 3 · 2028–2030+
Global HQ Campus

All ventures consolidated under one site. Thirteen buildings, three clusters. Externally financed from proven operations. The Founder's Office sits within as a personal command centre.

PHASE 4 · GENERATIONAL
Jolag Farm City

Large-scale agricultural city — food production, processing, housing, and industry. Institutional finance engaged. The billion-dollar layer — a city built around food and industry.

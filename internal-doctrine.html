OPERATING LAWS

Doctrine

The eight operating laws that govern how Komir Holdings builds, hires, enters markets, deploys capital, and makes decisions. These are not guidelines. They are the rules.

I
The Work Is the Pitch

Walk in with finished work. The quality of the deliverable creates the conversation. Never lead with price. Never wait for approval before starting. Build first. Deliver complete. Let the work do the talking.

II
One Shot, One Kill

The first delivery is production-ready. No shells. No placeholders. No "we'll refine later." Everything is built complete from the start. If it leaves Komir Holdings, it is finished.

III
The Engagement Filter

Every idea, client engagement, internal build, and feature request runs through five questions before it reaches the founder's desk.

01
Does this require the founder's mind specifically?
If a competent CTO could run it, it doesn't reach the desk.
02
Does this create IP that Komir owns?
If it doesn't produce a platform or licensable model, it's freelancing.
03
What does this unlock that isn't visible yet?
Every move should open a door behind it.
04
Is this a $10M conversation or a $10K conversation?
The founder has a floor. Every hour below it is stolen from above it.
05
Can this run without the founder?
If it can't be systematised and handed to a team, it's a job — not infrastructure.
IV
The First-Hire Doctrine

One person enters doing everything. They ascend as the next arrives. Nobody brought in above someone who grew from the ground. Character over credentials. Wholeness over intelligence.

V
Use What We Build

Every venture serves the others before it serves the market. The school educates our people. The media company tells our stories. The food brand feeds our teams. The portfolio is its own first and most demanding customer.

VI
Sovereign Capital

External capital is a strategic option from strength. Never a requirement for survival. The Capital Engine ensures every venture can be funded without waiting for permission from outside. When external capital enters, it enters on Komir's terms.

VII
The Enemy Is the System

The enemy is always the system, never a person. When something fails, the diagnosis points at the structure that allowed the failure, not the individual standing there when it broke. People are replaced when they cannot grow. Systems are rebuilt when they produce wrong outcomes.

VIII
The Country Ladder

Komir enters new countries through a five-rung sequence. No rung is skipped. Each one proves the market before the next investment.

RUNG 1
Country Lead deployed remotely. Mapping markets, identifying opportunities.
RUNG 2
Remote wins. Revenue generated before any physical presence.
RUNG 3
Local voice. Native content creator building visibility on the ground.
RUNG 4
Komir principal arrives. Revenue-funded. Flexible workspace.
RUNG 5
Country Lead appointed. Permanent presence formalised after in-person proof.

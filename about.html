About

A multi-venture holding company building the infrastructure that entire industries — and nations — run on.

THE COMPANY

Built from the inside. For the long term.

Komir Holdings is a privately held investment and holdings company headquartered in Nigeria. The portfolio spans technology, capital systems, education, government infrastructure, media, energy, and physical development — each venture designed to compound with the others as a single ecosystem.

The company is funded by a proprietary internal capital engine. External capital is a strategic option, never a dependency. Every venture is built to production standard before it meets the market, and every system generates the operational intelligence that makes the next build smarter.

Komir Holdings does not enter sectors to compete. It enters to build the foundational system each sector needs to function at scale — infrastructure that is designed for Nigeria first and deployable across Africa.

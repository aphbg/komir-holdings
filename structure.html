PORTFOLIO COMMAND

Group CEO

The Group CEO sits between the founder and all venture CEOs. This role commands the entire portfolio — translating strategic direction into operational targets, managing cross-venture dependencies, and ensuring every business executes to the standard the holding company demands.

This role is designed to be agent-first. The systems, decision frameworks, and operational playbooks are built to be run by AI before a human inherits the position. When the first human Group CEO arrives, they inherit a machine that already works — their job is to make it better, not to build it from scratch.

CAPITAL ARCHITECTURE

Director of Capital & Treasury

Routes Capital Engine output to ventures across the portfolio. Models cross-venture capitalisation — which businesses need capital, when, and in what form. Manages the treasury function that keeps sovereign capital flowing through the ecosystem without external dependency.

This role is the connection between the capital system and every operational entity. It ensures that capital allocation is strategic, not reactive — that every dollar deployed is building toward the portfolio's compounding architecture, not patching short-term gaps.

PERSONAL STAFF LAYER

The four roles that protect the founder's capacity.

The founder's time and cognitive bandwidth are the most valuable resources in the portfolio. These four roles exist to ensure that bandwidth is spent on what only the founder can do — vision, architecture, relationships, and strategic decisions. Everything else is handled.

Chief of Staff

The founder's brain externalised. Manages the decision queue, synthesises information across all ventures, and ensures the founder sees only what requires the founder's judgment. Reports directly to the founder. Every other staff role reports here.

Executive Assistant

The business brain. Manages the founder's schedule, communications, and operational rhythm. Handles the interface between the founder and the organisation. Reports to the Chief of Staff. First hire in the personal staff sequence.

Personal Assistant / Aide-de-Camp

Movement, travel, physical security, and logistics. Ensures the founder can move between locations, meetings, and events without friction. Manages the physical layer of the founder's daily operations.

House Manager

Domestic standard operating procedures. Manages the founder's personal environment — home, meals, maintenance, household staff, and personal logistics. The foundation that ensures the founder's non-work life runs without attention.

HIRING PHILOSOPHY

The First-Hire Doctrine.

This is how every team inside Komir Holdings is built. Not with org charts designed in advance, but with a principle that governs how people enter, grow, and lead.

One person enters doing everything. They ascend as the next arrives.

The first hire does everything — operations, admin, coordination, execution. They are not hired for a title. They are hired for their willingness to build from the ground.

As the next person arrives, the first hire ascends. They move up because they earned the ground beneath them. Nobody is ever brought in above someone who grew from the floor.

Character over credentials. A person's integrity, work ethic, and willingness to do what needs to be done outweighs any qualification, degree, or previous title.

Wholeness over intelligence. A brilliant person who cannot be trusted, who cannot collaborate, who cannot grow — is less valuable than a capable person who can do all three.

GOVERNANCE ARCHITECTURE

Five layers of structural protection.

Komir Holdings is built on intellectual property created by its founder. The governance architecture ensures that the relationship between the founder, the IP, and the company is protected permanently — through corporate structure, not through hope.

IP Origination

Every system, methodology, and architecture in the portfolio was created by the founder as personal intellectual property before being licensed to the holding company. The Capital Engine, every venture architecture, every operational system — all originated with the founder and are formally documented as such. The company operates on licensed IP. The IP belongs to the creator.

Dual-Class Share Structure

Two classes of shares. Class A carries standard voting rights. Class B carries superior voting rights and is held exclusively by the founder and designated trust structures. Class B shares are non-transferable and non-dilutable. Economic ownership can shift over time — voting control does not.

Founder Reserved Matters

A defined list of decisions that require the founder's written consent regardless of board vote or shareholder majority. This includes any sale, merger, or acquisition; new share issuance; changes to constitutional documents; Group CEO appointment; entry into new sectors; and any modification to the Capital Engine's deployment.

Irrevocable Trust

The family holding structure is held within an irrevocable trust. The trust holds Class B shares and the IP portfolio. It protects the structure from all parties — including the founder. The trustee's fiduciary duty is to the beneficiaries across generations, not to any individual, board, or external partner.

Golden Share

A single share with the power to block any resolution that would alter the company's fundamental character, ownership structure, or founder protections. Even if every other shareholder votes in favour and the board votes unanimously — the golden share holds the final authority.

BOARD STRATEGY

Five boards. Each composed for what the venture needs.

Board members are selected for what they bring to the specific venture — capital access, regulatory protection, industry credibility, or network multiplication. No advisory roles. Every seat carries responsibility.

Komir Holdings — Parent Board

The governing board for the entire portfolio. Oversees cross-venture strategy, capital allocation, governance standards, and long-term positioning. Composed of individuals with holding company experience, capital markets expertise, and multi-sector operational knowledge.
